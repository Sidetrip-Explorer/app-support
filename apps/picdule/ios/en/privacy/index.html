Picdule (iPhone) Privacy Policy

日本語

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy explains how Sidetrip Explorer ("we") handles information in Picdule, an iPhone app ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any information about users of the App on our servers or elsewhere. The App has no accounts or sign-in.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

  • Photos you take or choose and the events you add are stored in the App's private area on your device. They are not sent anywhere.
  • Reading text from photos (Apple Vision) and organizing event details (the Apple Intelligence model that runs on your device) both happen on your device.
  • This data is included in iOS device backups, such as iCloud Backup.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: used to take photos of flyers and schedules to add as events.
  • Photos: used to choose a photo of a flyer or schedule to add as an event.

4. Communication with Outside Services

Weather forecasts

  • The text in an event's place field is sent to Apple's map search service (MapKit), which returns its latitude, longitude, country, state or prefecture name, and time zone. For events without a place, the App sends "Tokyo" only when your device's region is Japan; in other regions it shows no weather.
  • That latitude and longitude are sent to Apple's weather service (WeatherKit), which returns a forecast for up to 10 days. Events more than 10 days away show no weather.
  • These services also receive your IP address as part of the connection.
  • The App does not access your current location (GPS) or the location data in your photos. Event titles, dates, notes, and photos are not sent.
  • Forecasts and search results are kept in the device's memory only while the App is running and only for the same day. They are not saved to files.
  • Tapping a weather badge opens a weather page for the place's country in a browser inside the App:
    • Japan: the weekly forecast page on tenki.jp for that prefecture. The place text and coordinates are not sent.
    • United States: the point forecast page of the National Weather Service (weather.gov). The latitude and longitude, rounded to two decimal places (about 1 km), are included in the page address.
    • Canada: the local forecast page of Environment and Climate Change Canada (weather.gc.ca). As in the United States, the rounded latitude and longitude are included in the page address.
    • Places in other countries do not open a page.
  • Tapping "Other data sources" next to " Weather" opens Apple's page listing its weather data sources.

Each service handles information according to its own privacy policy. We do not receive the contents of these communications.

Tips

  • You can leave a tip with an App Store in-app purchase from "Support the Developer (Tip)" in the settings. Tipping does not change any features or ads.
  • The App communicates with Apple's App Store to show tip prices and to make a purchase. Apple processes the payment; we do not receive your payment details or Apple Account information.
  • We receive sales totals from Apple. The App does not store purchase records.
  • Apple handles information according to Apple's privacy policy.

5. Advertising

  • The App shows Google AdMob ads at the bottom of the screen. For this, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects device identifiers, your IP address (used to estimate your approximate region), records of interactions such as ad views and taps, and diagnostic information, and sends them to Google. They are used to deliver ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud.
  • The App does not ask for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency), so it does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • In regions where consent is required, the App asks for your consent before showing ads. You can change your choices from "Ad Privacy Settings" in the App.
  • Google handles information according to Google's privacy policy.

6. Sharing with Third Parties

Because we do not hold any user information, we do not share any with third parties. The transmissions described in "4. Communication with Outside Services" and "5. Advertising" are made directly from your device to each service.

7. Retention and Deletion

When you delete an event, its photo is also deleted from your device. All photos and events stored on your device are deleted when you delete the App. Data included in device backups follows the handling of those backups.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date at the top.
