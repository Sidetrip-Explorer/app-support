Picdule is a calendar app that reads events from photos of flyers and posters. Instead of typing, just take or choose a photo, and Picdule reads the date, time, title, place, and more.

Main Features

  • Reading events from photos (date, time, title, and the 5W2H details: who, what, when, where, why, how, and how much)
  • Month, week, and day views, and event details shown with the photo
  • Adding events manually without a photo
  • Weather forecasts for each event's date and place (Japan, the United States, Canada, and more; up to 10 days ahead; data from Apple WeatherKit)
  • English and Japanese screens (English when your device's language is not Japanese)

Requirements

  • iPhone with iOS 17 or later. On models that support Apple Intelligence, the App uses AI to organize the text it reads into an event. On other models, it builds event suggestions from the text using rules. Either way, you can review and edit the details before adding the event.

Privacy

Photos and events are stored on your device, and both text reading and AI organizing happen on your device. For details, see the Picdule (iPhone) Privacy Policy.
