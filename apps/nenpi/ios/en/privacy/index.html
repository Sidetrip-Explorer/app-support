Picfuel (iPhone) Privacy Policy

日本語

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Sidetrip Explorer ("we") sets out below how user information is handled in the iPhone app "Picfuel" (the "App").

1. Information we collect

We do not collect information about users of the App on our servers or elsewhere. The App has no account registration or sign-in.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Your vehicles, fuel records (date and time, odometer reading, amount of fuel, unit price, total, fuel type, brand, station name and address) and settings (including your units) are stored in the App's own area on your device. They are not sent anywhere.
  • Receipt images attached to fuel records are stored in the App's own area on your device. Images are not sent anywhere.
  • Text recognition on receipts (Apple Vision) and extraction of values such as the amount of fuel and the total (including, where used, the Apple Intelligence model that runs on your device) happen on your device.
  • When you choose a receipt photo, the App reads the date the photo was taken from the photo's information, on your device, to fill in the date when it can't be read from the receipt. Location and other photo information are not used.
  • The App reads your iPhone's language and region on your device to choose the screen language, the default units and how receipts are read. They are not sent anywhere.
  • This data is included in backups of your device made by iOS, such as iCloud Backup.
  • When you tap "Export" in Settings, all vehicles and fuel records (without receipt images) are written to one file and handed to the save location or sharing destination you choose. The App never sends this file anywhere on its own.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: used to photograph receipts.
  • Photos: used to choose a photo of a receipt.

4. Communication with outside services

  • Except for the tip jar below and the ads described in "5. Advertising", the App does not communicate with outside services. Fuel records and receipt images and their contents are never sent.
  • When you tap "Feedback, requests and bug reports" in Settings, the App creates an email to us in your device's Mail app. You decide whether to send it.

Tip jar

  • You can leave a tip through an App Store in-app purchase from "Support the Developer (Tip)" in Settings. Tipping doesn't change any features or ads.
  • When showing tip prices and when you buy a tip, the App communicates with Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we do not receive your payment information or Apple Account information.
  • We receive sales totals from Apple. The App does not store a record of purchases.
  • Apple's handling of information is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

5. Advertising

  • The App shows Google AdMob ads at the top of the screen. For this, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects device identifiers, your IP address (used to estimate your approximate location), records of interactions such as ad views and taps, and diagnostic information, and sends them to Google. They are used to serve ads, measure ad performance and prevent fraud.
  • The advertising identifier (IDFA) is used for ads based on your interests only if you allow tracking (App Tracking Transparency). You can change this at any time in the iPhone Settings app under Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • In regions where consent is required, consent is requested before ads are shown. You can change your consent with "Change ad consent" in the App's Settings.
  • Google's handling of information is governed by Google's privacy policy.

6. Sharing with third parties

Because we do not hold any user information, we do not provide any information to third parties. The transmissions described under the tip jar in "4. Communication with outside services" and in "5. Advertising" are made by the App directly from your device to Apple and Google.

7. Retention and deletion

  • When you delete a fuel record, its receipt image is also deleted from your device. When you retake a receipt photo, the previous image is deleted.
  • When you delete a vehicle, its fuel records and receipt images are deleted.
  • When you tap "Delete all data" in Settings, all vehicles, fuel records, settings and receipt images are deleted.
  • When you import a backup file with "Import" in Settings, the data on your device is replaced with the contents of the file, and the previous receipt images are deleted.
  • All data stored on your device is deleted when you delete the App from your device. Data included in device backups and exported files are handled according to where they are stored.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date at the top.
