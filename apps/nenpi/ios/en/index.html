Picfuel (iPhone) Support

日本語

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Picfuel is a fuel log that calculates your fuel economy from each fill-up. Take a photo of your gas station receipt, or choose one from your library, and Picfuel reads the amount of fuel, the price per gallon or liter, the total and the date for you.

Features

  • Reads fuel receipts from the United States, Canada and Japan: amount of fuel (gallons or liters), unit price, total, date and fuel brand. If a value is read wrong, tap "From Photo" on that field and pick the right text on the receipt
  • If the date can't be read from the receipt, the date the photo was taken is used, and the app tells you so
  • Choose your units: miles or kilometers, gallons or liters, mpg, mi/L, km/L or L/100km, and dollars or yen. If the receipt uses a different unit, the values are converted to your units and the app tells you
  • Saves the receipt image with the fuel record, so you can look at it or read it again later
  • Totals for fuel economy, distance, fuel and cost, and a chart of your fuel economy over time
  • Spots fill-ups where the fuel economy jumps much higher than usual, and lets you know a fill-up may be missing
  • Several vehicles, each with its own records and fuel economy
  • Enter the trip distance instead of the odometer reading, and a large text option
  • Export all records to a file and import them again (for example, when you move to a new iPhone)
  • English and Japanese screens (English when your iPhone's language is not Japanese)
  • An optional tip jar to support the developer. Tipping doesn't change any features or ads

Requirements

  • iPhone with iOS 16.4 or later. Receipts are read with built-in rules for your region. For Japanese receipts, on iPhones with iOS 26 or later where Apple Intelligence is turned on, the on-device model also double-checks the values read (an "AI" badge appears next to the title). Either way, you can check and correct everything before you save.

Privacy

Fuel records and receipt images are stored on your device, and text reading (and AI checking, where used) happens on your device. The app communicates with outside services only to show ads and to show prices for and process tips (Apple's App Store). For details, see the Picfuel (iPhone) Privacy Policy.
